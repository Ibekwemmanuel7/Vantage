INDEPENDENT PROPOSAL · 20 OCTOBER 2026

Vantage

Satellite evidence for better extension field questions.

Prepared for discussion with David S. Vanderpool. His role and intended organization must be established. No IDEA representation, IDEA/LiveBeyond relationship, approval or engagement is assumed.

The decision

Which agricultural areas should extension teams investigate this week, and what evidence supports that priority?

The proposed contribution

A weekly evidence insert for expert review: dated vegetation and rainfall measurements, locations and scale, historical comparisons, usable coverage, source references and explicit unknowns. Qualified agricultural experts interpret the evidence and choose local checks. File delivery can precede any system integration.

What can be demonstrated now

A local, unpublished Iganga-area research prototype using real public Sentinel-2, historical WorldCover cropland and CHIRPS rainfall. The May 2024 reconstruction flags low five-day rainfall against its selected earlier-year reference, while withholding vegetation assessments in three of four units for insufficient coverage. The one usable vegetation unit is close to its reference. This establishes neither maize damage nor a cause, timely historical alerting or customer performance.

A bounded first pilot

One jointly selected district/subset and a maize-oriented extension workflow; setup followed by six weekly review cycles. Deliver a printable brief, CSV, GeoJSON and structured expert/field feedback. Confirm district boundaries, crop locations, season, editorial cutoff and field capacity before operating. No private farmer database or AgriPortal API access is assumed.

How we would assess usefulness

Useful field checks, confirmed and misleading flags, findings in sampled unflagged areas, analyst time, turnaround, coverage and usage. Record the intended checks before revealing Vantage's priorities. No promised yield gains, food-security improvements, imagery-only diagnosis or treatment advice.

Questions for the discussion

In what capacity are you reviewing this, and which organization/program would use it? Who owns the decision, expert review and budget? Which district/crop has a pressing question and feasible checks? What would justify a paid trial? Is the next step a technical review, a scoped proposal or an introduction to the appropriate owner?

Scope and pricing remain hypotheses to cost against analyst effort, data processing and partner logistics. No offer has been accepted; nothing has been sent or published by this development work.
