FROM THE SCREEN TO THE FIELD

A clearer path
to a closer look.

Designed around the people who know the land. Each step keeps the observations and their limitations visible.

  1. 01

    Know the fields.

    Import GeoJSON boundaries into a browser-local register. Keep crop identity and planting dates explicit and unverified until confirmed.

    Explore the field register ↗
  2. 02

    Question the change.

    Review dated locality imagery and usable coverage. A newer acquisition can still be cloudy, missing or unsuitable for interpretation.

    Review observation dates ↗

    Keep the evidence.

    Prepare a local review checklist and export a dated brief. Draft notes stay separate from independently verified field outcomes.

WHERE THE PRODUCT STANDS

Available in the demonstration

Historical locality imagery, quality and staleness context, browser-local boundaries and review notes, and downloadable evidence briefs.

Needed for an operational pilot

Consented and verified fields, confirmed crop calendars, current observations, independent dated field outcomes, secure accounts and durable data synchronization.

What imagery alone cannot establish

A cause of crop stress, a disease diagnosis, an irrigation prescription or a validated production yield forecast. Field investigation and independent evaluation remain essential.
