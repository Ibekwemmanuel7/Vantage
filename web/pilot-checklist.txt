VANTAGE — AGRICULTURE PILOT DISCUSSION CHECKLIST

Purpose: define one useful field decision and the evidence needed to evaluate it.
This is a planning checklist, not a service agreement or monitoring commitment.

1. PEOPLE AND DECISION
Who uses the observations? Who makes the field decision? Who would pay?
Which fields are already due for inspection, and what would prioritisation change?

2. LOCAL CONTEXT
Proposed country, district and bounded study area:
Crop, season and management system:
Confirmed planting dates and crop-stage records available:

3. CONSENT AND FIELD RECORDS
Who can supply and authorise use of field boundaries?
How will confidential records be stored and access controlled?
Who can supply dated, independent scouting findings and outcomes?

4. OBSERVATION FEASIBILITY
Required decision cadence and maximum acceptable observation age:
Expected cloud, small-field and mixed-pixel limitations:
Public data available; commercial data costs and reuse permissions if needed:
What happens when observations are missing, cloudy or stale?

5. EVALUATION BEFORE CLAIMS
Predefine the decision, baseline, independent outcomes and success criteria.
Separate development and evaluation fields, locations and seasons.
Measure usable coverage, lead time, false flags, missed outcomes and field usefulness.
Do not infer disease causes or prescribe irrigation from imagery alone.

6. CURRENT DEMONSTRATION
Historical Kween/Ngenge locality context: evidence cutoff 5 June 2023.
No verified field-level recommendations; zero independent field outcomes.
Browser-local field register and draft notes; no account or cloud synchronization.
Operational deployment requires security, current data and prospective validation.
