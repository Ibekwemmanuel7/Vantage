# Vantage: a proposed satellite evidence service for extension teams

Prepared for a discussion with David S. Vanderpool · 20 October 2026

**Purpose:** explore whether a weekly, expert-reviewed satellite evidence insert
could support the relevant organization's agricultural reporting and extension
work. This is an independent proposal. David's role and intended organization
must be established; no IDEA representation, LiveBeyond relationship, approval
or engagement is assumed.

**Question supported:** Which agricultural areas should extension teams
investigate this week, and what evidence supports that priority?

**Proposed contribution:** Vantage supplies location/scale, vegetation and rainfall
measurements, dates, coverage, historical comparisons, provenance and explicit
unknowns. Qualified experts interpret them and choose the field questions.
Weekly report files can be delivered without assuming access to private systems.

**Demonstration available:** a local, unpublished Iganga-area research prototype
using real public Sentinel-2, historical WorldCover cropland and CHIRPS rainfall.
The May 2024 reconstruction flags low five-day rainfall relative to its selected
earlier-year reference, while explicitly withholding vegetation assessments in
three of four units for insufficient usable coverage. The one usable vegetation
unit is close to its reference. This does not establish maize damage, a cause,
timely historical alerting or customer performance.

**Proposed pilot:** one jointly chosen district/subset, a maize-focused extension
workflow, setup and six weekly review cycles; printable evidence brief, CSV,
GeoJSON and structured review/field feedback. Crop location, season and district
boundaries require confirmation. No private farmer records are needed for the
first research demonstration.

**What to measure:** usefulness of checks, confirmed and misleading flags,
findings in sampled unflagged areas, analyst time, turnaround, coverage and usage.
No promised yield gains, food-security outcomes, diagnosis or treatment advice.

**Discussion questions:** In what capacity are you reviewing this, and which
organization/program would use it? Who owns the weekly decision, editorial review
and budget? Which district/crop has both a pressing question and feasible field
checks? What would make a brief useful enough to trial? Would a next step be a
technical review, a scoped proposal, or an introduction to the appropriate owner?

**Commercial position:** paid pilot scope and price remain hypotheses, to be
costed against analyst effort, data processing and partner logistics. No offer
has been accepted. Nothing has been sent or published by this development work.
