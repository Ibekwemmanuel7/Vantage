# Demonstration script and discovery guide

## Five-minute demonstration

1. Start `python scripts/serve_demo.py --port 8917` from the repository and open
   http://127.0.0.1:8917. No network is needed for cached data. State that the
   demonstration is retrospective, independent and unapproved by IDEA.
2. Show the Uganda profile, study geometry/scale and 20 May 2024 cutoff. Explain
   that maize is the proposed extension workflow, not a satellite-confirmed crop.
3. Select IG-11. Compare the rainfall finding with near-reference NDVI. Explain
   that low rainfall does not automatically mean crop damage or irrigation need.
4. Select IG-12. Show the insufficient vegetation coverage alongside the separate
   rainfall signal. Filter Evidence gaps; these areas are still visible even when
   the rainfall rule places them in review. Explain the reference and source dates.
5. Open the weekly printable brief; download CSV/GeoJSON. Record a clearly labeled
   demonstration review, reload and show persistence. No actual historical field
   check has been established by the demonstration entry.
6. Switch to Texas. It is a separate synthetic cotton fixture and engine policy,
   not transferred Uganda validation. Return to Uganda for the discussion.

## Discovery questions

- What is your role, and which organization/program is considering the service?
- Who would use the weekly queue, review interpretation and authorize payment?
- Which district, crop and seasonal decision should the first pilot address?
- How are weekly reports prepared today? What takes time, and what is missing?
- Which spatial scale is useful to extension staff? Are area-level checks viable?
- What local crop, planting/stage and dated field references are available legally?
- How often can staff check flagged and sampled unflagged areas, and log no visits?
- What is the editorial cutoff, acceptable data lag and preferred delivery format?
- Does an existing tool already meet the need? What improvement would justify cost?
- Are file exports sufficient? If an API is useful, who owns it and can document it?
- What are the budget process, procurement timeline and next review step?
- Which data can be retained, and who can approve a public retrospective case study?

Do not infer roles or authority from a name or prior contact. Do not promise
agronomic outcomes or present this as an approved IDEA/LiveBeyond partnership.

## Demonstration labels

Uganda: cached real-public-data retrospective reconstruction; method development,
no independent field validation. Texas: synthetic future-season fixture. Saved
reviews: demonstration feedback only. Area draft: an unprocessed configuration,
not a newly analyzed area. The UI uses no live-data fallback.
